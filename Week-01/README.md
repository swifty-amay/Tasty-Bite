## 🌟 Week Overview

Welcome to Week 1 of our development journey!

This week focuses on understanding the requirements, objectives, and overall workflow of Tasty Bite, our Smart Food Ordering System.

As the Backend Manager, my responsibility is to understand the system requirements, identify backend functionalities, coordinate backend tasks, and help establish a clear development plan.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand the project's requirements.
- Identify the major system functionalities.
- Understand the responsibilities of backend development.
- Identify the data required by the application.
- Coordinate with team members regarding backend tasks.

## 💡 Key Areas of Focus

### 📋 1. Requirement Analysis
Understanding the functional requirements of the food ordering system.

### ⚙️ 2. Backend Responsibilities
Identifying the server-side functionalities required by the application.

### 🗄️ 3. Data Requirements
Identifying information related to food items, categories, customers, and orders.

### 👥 4. Team Coordination
Understanding team responsibilities and planning backend-related activities.

### 📝 5. Project Planning
Preparing an initial development roadmap for the project.

## 👩‍💻 My Role: Backend Manager

- Analyze backend requirements.
- Coordinate backend-related tasks.
- Help define the backend development workflow.
- Track assigned tasks and project progress.
- Communicate backend requirements to team members.
