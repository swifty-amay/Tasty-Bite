# Week 1: The Beginning of Our Journey!
Every great project starts with a great idea! 🚀
Welcome to **Food Coders**, our project focused on developing **Tasty Bite**, a Smart Food Ordering System.

Our goal is to create a convenient and user-friendly application that makes food ordering simpler and helps organize food items, categories, and orders efficiently.

This week marks the beginning of our 12-week development journey. We focused on understanding the project requirements, identifying the main objectives, and planning the features we aim to develop.
# Project Objectives :

1. Develop a user-friendly food ordering application.
2. Organize food items into different categories.
3. Simplify food selection and ordering.
4. Manage food-related information using a database.
5. Build a structured and reliable application.
