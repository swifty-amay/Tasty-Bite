## 🚀 Week 2: Planning the Foundation

Welcome back to our development journey! 💻

In Week 2, we are focusing on planning the structure and workflow of **Tasty Bite**, our Smart Food Ordering System. A clear development plan helps us organize the project and prepare for the upcoming implementation stages.

## 🎯 This Week's Objectives

* Define the application's basic workflow.
* Identify the responsibilities of different modules.
* Plan how food categories and food items will be organized.
* Understand the database requirements.
* Prepare for the development phase.

## 💡 What We're Working On

📌 **Application Workflow**
Planning how users will navigate through the food ordering application.

🍕 **Food Categories**
Organizing food items into suitable categories.

🛒 **Shopping Cart Planning**
Identifying the basic operations required to manage selected food items.

🗄️ **Database Planning**
Identifying the information that needs to be stored in the database.

💻 **Project Structure**
Planning the organization of Java classes and application components in Eclipse IDE.
