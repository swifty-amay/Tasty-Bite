# 🍽️ FOOD CODERS
## **Project Title: Tasty Bite**
### 🗄️ Week 3: Database Design & Planning
## 🌟 Week Overview

Welcome to Week 3 of our development journey!

This week focuses on planning the database structure for **Tasty Bite**, our Smart Food Ordering System. A well-organized database helps store and manage food items, categories, and order information efficiently.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify the data required by the application.
* Plan the database structure.
* Identify the necessary database tables.
* Understand the relationships between different tables.
* Prepare for database implementation using MySQL.

## 💡 Key Areas of Focus

### 🗂️ 1. Database Structure

Planning how application data will be organized and stored.

### 🍕 2. Food Category Table

Identifying the information needed to organize food items into categories.

### 🍔 3. Food Items Table

Planning how food names, prices, descriptions, and category details will be stored.

### 📋 4. Orders Table

Identifying the information required to maintain customer orders.

### 🔗 5. Table Relationships

Understanding how food categories, food items, and orders relate to one another.
