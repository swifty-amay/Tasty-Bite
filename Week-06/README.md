# 🍽️ FOOD CODERS
## **Project Title: Tasty Bite**
### 🍔 Week 6: Food Item Management
## 🌟 Week Overview

Welcome to Week 6 of our 12-week development journey! 🚀

This week focuses on food item management in **Tasty Bite**, our Smart Food Ordering System. The goal is to organize food item information and make it easier to maintain details such as food names, prices, descriptions, and categories.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Organize food item information.
* Manage food names, prices, and descriptions.
* Associate food items with suitable categories.
* Plan operations for adding, viewing, updating, and deleting food records.
* Improve the organization of food menu data.

## 💡 Key Areas of Focus

### 🍕 1. Food Item Details

Identifying the information required for each food item, including its name, price, and category.

### 🗂️ 2. Category Management

Organizing food items into appropriate categories for easier browsing.

### ✏️ 3. Adding and Updating Items

Planning how new food items can be added and existing details updated.

### 🗑️ 4. Removing Items

Planning how unavailable or unnecessary food items can be removed.

### 🗄️ 5. Database Management

Working toward storing and retrieving food item information through MySQL.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* ☕ Java
* 🎨 Java Swing
* 🗄️ MySQL
* 🔗 JDBC
* 💻 Eclipse IDE
* 🐙 GitHub
