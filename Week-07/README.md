# 🍽️ FOOD CODERS
## **Project Title: Tasty Bite**
### 🛒 Week 7: Shopping Cart Development
## 🌟 Week Overview

Welcome to Week 7 of our 12-week development journey! 🚀

This week focuses on the shopping cart functionality of **Tasty Bite**, our Smart Food Ordering System. The shopping cart is designed to help users select food items, manage their selections, and calculate the total cost before placing an order.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Plan the shopping cart functionality.
* Allow users to select food items.
* Manage the quantity of selected items.
* Calculate the total price of selected food items.
* Provide options to update or remove items from the cart.

## 💡 Key Areas of Focus

### 🍔 1. Adding Food Items

Planning how users can add their selected food items to the cart.

### 🔢 2. Quantity Management

Managing the quantity of each selected food item.

### 💰 3. Total Price Calculation

Calculating the total cost based on item prices and quantities.

### 🗑️ 4. Removing Items

Allowing unwanted food items to be removed from the cart.

### 🎨 5. Cart Interface

Planning a clear and user-friendly shopping cart interface using Java Swing.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* 🗄️ MySQL
* 🔗 JDBC
* 💻 Eclipse IDE
