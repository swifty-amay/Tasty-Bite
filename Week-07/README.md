# 🍔 Week 7: Food & Category Management Backend

## 🌟 Week Overview

Welcome to Week 7 of our development journey!

This week focuses on developing and coordinating backend operations for food categories and food items.

These functionalities allow the application to organize menu information and retrieve food details from the database.

As the Backend Manager, I will coordinate the development of food-related operations and help ensure that the backend follows the planned database structure.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Implement backend operations for food categories.
- Manage food item information.
- Retrieve food details from MySQL.
- Support adding and updating food records.
- Validate food-related information.

## 💡 Key Areas of Focus

### 🍕 1. Category Management
Organizing food items into appropriate categories.

### 🍔 2. Food Item Management
Managing food names, descriptions, prices, and category information.

### 📥 3. Food Data Retrieval
Retrieving menu information from the database.

### ✏️ 4. Record Updates
Preparing operations for updating food information.

### 🗑️ 5. Record Deletion
Supporting removal of food records when permitted by the application design.

### ✅ 6. Data Validation
Checking required fields and ensuring valid food prices and category information.

## 👩‍💻 My Role: Backend Manager

- Coordinate food-management backend tasks.
- Review food and category data operations.
- Track development progress.
- Help identify database and query issues.
- Coordinate integration requirements with team members.
