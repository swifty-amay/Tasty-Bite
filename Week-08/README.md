# 📋 Week 8: Customer & Order Management

## 🌟 Week Overview

Welcome to Week 8 of our development journey!

This week focuses on customer-related data and order management operations in Tasty Bite.

The backend must handle customer information and maintain accurate order records to support the food ordering workflow.

As the Backend Manager, I will coordinate the development of these functionalities and help ensure that customer and order information is handled consistently.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Identify the required customer information.
- Develop customer-related database operations.
- Plan the order creation workflow.
- Store order information in MySQL.
- Maintain relationships between customers and orders.

## 💡 Key Areas of Focus

### 👤 1. Customer Management
Organizing the customer information required by the application.

### 📋 2. Order Creation
Preparing backend operations for creating customer orders.

### 🔗 3. Customer-Order Relationships
Associating orders with the appropriate customer records.

### 📦 4. Order Details
Maintaining information about ordered food items and quantities.

### 💰 5. Order Amount
Preparing the calculation and storage of order totals.

### ✅ 6. Data Consistency
Ensuring that order information is stored accurately.

## 👩‍💻 My Role: Backend Manager

- Coordinate customer and order management tasks.
- Review database operations related to orders.
- Track backend development progress.
- Help identify order-processing issues.
- Coordinate testing and integration requirements.
