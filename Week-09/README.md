# 🛒 Week 9: Order Processing & Business Logic

## 🌟 Week Overview

Welcome to Week 9 of our development journey!

This week focuses on the business logic required to process food orders in Tasty Bite.

The backend must calculate order totals, verify food information, manage quantities, and maintain accurate order records.

As the Backend Manager, I will coordinate order-processing tasks and help ensure that the business logic works consistently with the database.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Define the order-processing workflow.
- Calculate food item totals and order amounts.
- Validate quantities and food availability.
- Store order information in the database.
- Maintain accurate order details.

## 💡 Key Areas of Focus

### 🛒 1. Order Workflow
Defining how selected food items become an order.

### 🔢 2. Quantity Management
Handling the quantity of each selected food item.

### 💰 3. Price Calculation
Calculating item subtotals and the total order amount.

### ✅ 4. Input Validation
Checking quantities, prices, and required order information.

### 🗄️ 5. Database Operations
Saving order information and related order details.

### 📋 6. Order Status
Planning how order statuses will be maintained if supported by the application.

## 👩‍💻 My Role: Backend Manager

- Coordinate order-processing development.
- Review business logic and calculations.
- Track order-related backend tasks.
- Help identify calculation and data-consistency issues.
- Coordinate testing of the order workflow.
